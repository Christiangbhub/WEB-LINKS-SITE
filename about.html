Welcome to the About Page

Hi, I’m Christian George — a web developer, graphic designer, and Computer Programming student based in Canada. I enjoy building clean, functional digital experiences and visually appealing designs. Whether it’s a website, a flyer, a poster, or a small full-stack app, I love creating things that look good and work smoothly.

I graduated at Sheridan college with a Computer Programming diploma, where I have been developing strong skills in Java, JavaScript, Angular, React/Next.js, SQL, Git, and building real applications like REST APIs with Spring Boot. On the design side, I use Photoshop and Canva to create professional graphics and layouts for practice, personal projects, and clients. Outside school, I spend time building projects on GitHub, improving my design portfolio on Behance, and learning new technologies that help me grow as a developer and designer.

I aim to combining good code and great design — to create simple, modern, and user-friendly work.
